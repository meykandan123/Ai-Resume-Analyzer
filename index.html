What Makes a Strong Resume

  • Clear contact info — name, email, phone, and a LinkedIn/GitHub link at the top.
  • Quantified achievements — use numbers and results, not just duties.
  • Relevant skills — match keywords from the job description for ATS scanning.
  • Action verbs — start bullet points with words like "led," "built," "improved."
  • Concise length — ideally one page, focused on the most relevant experience.
  • Clean formatting — consistent fonts, no tables/graphics that confuse ATS parsers.
  • No errors — proofread for spelling, grammar, and consistent tense.
  • Tailored content — customize for each role instead of sending one generic version.
