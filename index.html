What Makes a Strong Resume

  • Clear contact info — name, email, phone, and a LinkedIn/GitHub link at the top.
  • Quantified achievements — use numbers and results, not just duties.
  • Relevant skills — match keywords from the job description for ATS scanning.
  • Action verbs — start bullet points with words like "led," "built," "improved."
  • Concise length — ideally one page, focused on the most relevant experience.
  • Clean formatting — consistent fonts, no tables/graphics that confuse ATS parsers.
  • No errors — proofread for spelling, grammar, and consistent tense.
  • Tailored content — customize for each role instead of sending one generic version.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Everything you need to know about our AI resume scanner, ATS scoring system, and keyword optimization engine.

      What is an ATS resume score?

      An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) score is an estimated percentage measuring how effectively your resume is structured, parsed, and keyword-matched by automated screening software used by over 98% of Fortune 500 companies.

      How does the AI Resume Analyzer evaluate my resume?

      The analyzer parses your resume (PDF, DOCX, or text) directly in your browser, extracts key sections (contact information, skills, experience, projects, education), checks for ATS-friendly formatting, and matches industry keywords and job posting requirements.

      Is the AI Resume Analyzer free to use?

      Yes, AI Resume Analyzer is 100% free to use. You can upload or paste your resume, calculate your ATS score, discover missing keywords, and download detailed PDF evaluation reports at zero cost.

      Does the AI Resume Analyzer store or share my personal data?

      Your privacy is strictly respected. Initial document text parsing and keyword matching take place locally in your browser. User account history is securely encrypted and never shared or sold to third parties.

      What file formats are supported?

      The analyzer natively supports PDF documents (.pdf), Microsoft Word files (.docx), and plain text copy-pasting, preserving layout hierarchy and parsing two-column formats accurately.

      Can I compare my resume against a specific job role?

      Yes! With the 'ATS with Role' feature, you can select your target position and paste the job description to receive a tailored keyword match score, missing skills breakdown, and personalized recommendations.

      AI Resume Analyzer was created and developed by Meykandan as an open-access, browser-powered tool to help job seekers optimize their resumes, pass automated ATS screenings, and secure top interviews.
